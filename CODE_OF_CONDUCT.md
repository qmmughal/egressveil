# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this community a harassment-free experience
for everyone. That applies regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project's users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
  This includes credentials or personal data found in logs or bug reports.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code. They may remove,
edit or reject contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors.

To report a conduct problem, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/qmmughal/egressveil/security/advisories/new)
and put "Code of Conduct" in the title, or by any other private channel the
maintainers list in the repository. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including its enforcement
guidelines, applies and is available at the link above.
